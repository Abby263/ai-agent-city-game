# Nakameguro: A Real Tokyo Neighbourhood

The town is a stylised, scaled-down Nakameguro (Meguro City, Tokyo). It is not a map-accurate model: it keeps the
neighbourhood's shape and landmarks so the game feels like a real place.

## Geography

| In the game | Real place |
| --- | --- |
| River through the middle, lined with cherry trees | The Meguro River, famous for its sakura and lanterns in spring |
| West bank: houses, school, park | Aobadai: hilly residential streets and Saigoyama Park |
| East bank: station, shops, towers | Kamimeguro around Nakameguro Station |
| Elevated railway with trains | The Tōyoko line viaduct through Nakameguro Station |
| Shops under the tracks | Nakameguro Kōkashita, the arcade beneath the viaduct |
| Hikawa Shrine | Kamimeguro Hikawa Shrine |
| Meguro City Office, kōban, Kyosai Hospital, GT Tower | Real public landmarks near the station |

Private businesses (Sunny Side Cafe, HappyMart 24, Minami Clinic, Aobadai Bank) and every resident are fictional.

## Real Time, Date And Weather

- **Live mode (default):** the clock is real Tokyo time. Night in Tokyo is night in the game. Returning after a break
  catches the town up: each missed day is lived (birthdays, illness, bills), then everyone is placed where they'd be now.
  With Auto on, conversations happen about every 40 seconds.
- **Fast-forward (1x/2x/4x):** watch days pass quickly. Switching back to Live resumes real time.
- **Calendar:** real dates, seasons, Japanese public holidays and school breaks (summer, winter, spring).
- **Weather** follows Tokyo's climate: monthly temperatures, tsuyu rainy season, summer heatwaves and thunderstorms,
  typhoons (Aug-Oct), winter snow, morning fog and earthquakes. "Live Tokyo weather" in Create copies today's real
  weather from Open-Meteo.
- **Seasons show:** cherry blossoms and river lanterns in late March and early April, fresh leaves, summer green,
  red and gold autumn leaves, bare winter branches.

## What Weather Does

Rain moves people indoors (kids to the arcade, seniors to the library) and opens umbrellas. Snow brings snowmen and
slow walking. Heatwaves send people into air-conditioning, and older people risk heatstroke. Typhoons close school
and shops, suspend trains and buses, and keep everyone home except essential workers. Strong earthquakes shake the
camera, cause a few minor injuries and send everyone to the school evacuation area. Winter is flu season.
The AI residents know the date, time, weather and any alerts, so they talk about them.

## Playing God And Taking Action

- **Create:** change the weather, trigger an earthquake, hold a neighbourhood-association election, or write anything
  that happens ("a water pipe bursts at the library", "Yui gets a job offer in Osaka"). The first reaction plays at once
  and the **Happening now** card tracks what follows.
- **Act (on any profile) and the quick bar:** make anyone do anything to anyone, in your own words. There is no list of
  actions: the game master reads the words, capped effects follow (feelings, onlookers, injuries, money, closures), and
  the people involved react in their own AI-generated words. See [player control](player-control.md).
- Everyone is an adult. Conflict is non-graphic and has consequences; romance between relatives is refused, and a date,
  engagement or moving in only happens if the other person says yes.
